# Getting Started with Lumen Desk

## Create your workspace

Sign up with your work email, then choose **Create a new workspace**.
Pick a workspace name (usually your company name) and a unique subdomain
such as `yourcompany.lumendesk.app`. The person who creates the workspace
becomes its first admin.

## Invite your team

Go to **Settings → Team members → Invite**. Enter email addresses and
choose a role for each person:

- **Admin** — full access, including billing and workspace settings.
- **Agent** — can answer customers and edit their own profile.
- **Viewer** — read-only access to conversations and reports.

Invitations expire after 7 days. If a teammate never receives the invite
email, ask them to check spam, or resend it from the same screen.

## Connect your first inbox

Go to **Settings → Inboxes → Connect inbox**. You can forward your
existing support address (for example support@yourcompany.com) to the
Lumen Desk forwarding address shown on screen, or connect a Google
Workspace or Microsoft 365 mailbox directly with OAuth sign-in. New
emails appear in the shared inbox within about a minute.

## Import existing contacts and history

Under **Settings → Data → Import**, upload a CSV of contacts with the
columns `name`, `email`, and optionally `company` and `phone`. A typical
import of 10,000 contacts finishes in under 10 minutes. You will get an
email summary listing any rows that were skipped because of invalid
email addresses.

## Your first week checklist

1. Create the workspace and invite at least one admin besides yourself.
2. Connect your support mailbox and send a test email to verify routing.
3. Create five saved replies for your most common questions.
4. Turn on the business-hours automation so customers get an honest
   reply expectation outside working hours.
