# Billing and Plans

## Plan prices

Prices are per user, per month, billed in USD:

| Plan       | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) |
|------------|-----------------|----------------------------|
| Free       | $0              | $0                         |
| Starter    | $12             | $10                        |
| Pro        | $29             | $24                        |
| Enterprise | Custom          | Custom                     |

The Free plan includes 1 inbox and up to 3 users. Starter adds unlimited
inboxes and saved replies. Pro adds automations, integrations, SSO, and
advanced reporting. Enterprise adds a dedicated account manager, custom
data-retention terms, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Billing cycle and invoices

Paid plans renew automatically on the same day each month (or each year
for annual billing). Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and are
always available under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**, where you can
download them as PDF. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and
ACH transfers on Enterprise plans.

## Changing plans

Admins can upgrade or downgrade under **Settings → Billing → Change
plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately and we charge a prorated amount
for the rest of the cycle. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal,
and features above the new plan's limits become read-only until then.

## Refund policy

If you cancel within the first 30 days of a new paid plan, contact
support and we will refund the first payment in full. After 30 days,
payments are non-refundable, but you keep access until the end of the
paid period. Annual plans cancelled mid-year are refunded on a prorated
basis minus the months already used at the monthly rate.

## Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, we retry the card over 7 days and email the
billing contact after each attempt. The workspace stays fully usable
during the first 7 days; after that it becomes read-only until payment
succeeds. No customer data is deleted for failed payments.
