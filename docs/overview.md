# Lumen Desk Overview

Lumen Desk is a shared-inbox and customer support platform for small and
mid-sized teams. Instead of every agent working from a personal email
client, your whole support team works from one collaborative inbox with
assignments, internal notes, and a shared view of every customer
conversation.

## Core features

- **Shared inbox** — email, chat, and social messages in one queue, with
  collision detection so two agents never answer the same customer.
- **Assignments and mentions** — route conversations to an owner and pull
  teammates in with @mentions and private notes.
- **Saved replies** — reusable response templates your team can insert
  and personalize in one click.
- **Automations** — rules that tag, assign, or close conversations based
  on sender, keywords, or business hours.
- **Reports** — response time, resolution time, and conversation volume
  dashboards, exportable to CSV.

## Who uses Lumen Desk

Support teams of 2 to 200 agents typically use Lumen Desk: e-commerce
stores, SaaS companies, agencies, and IT help desks. A workspace is the
top-level container; one company usually has one workspace with several
inboxes inside it (for example Support, Sales, and Billing).

## Plans at a glance

Lumen Desk comes in four plans: Free, Starter, Pro, and Enterprise.
Pro is the most popular plan because it unlocks automations, integrations,
and advanced reporting. See the Billing and Plans document for exact
prices and billing rules.
